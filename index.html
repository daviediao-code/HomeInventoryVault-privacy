Privacy Policy

Home Inventory Vault

Effective date: November 5, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 5, 2026
Published by AstraCore Studio  ·  Android app package: com.homeinventoryvault.app

1The short version

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal information.
  • Your inventory, photos, documents, warranties and notes stay on your device.
  • There is no account, no cloud sync, no advertising and no analytics.
  • The only network connection is Google Play Billing for the optional one-time Pro purchase.
  • You control every backup file the app creates.

2Information you create in the app

You may enter item names, prices, purchase dates, serial numbers, warranty details, notes, photos and documents. This content is stored in the app's private storage on your device and in the app's local database.

We do not have access to this content. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, the content is deleted from the device.

3Photos, documents and camera

You can attach photos and documents to items. The app copies the files you select into its private storage so that they remain available. The app does not scan your photo library in the background.

The barcode scanner uses Google Play Services' on-device code scanner. The camera is used only while the scanner screen is open. Scanned barcode values are stored locally with the item you choose. The scanner may download an on-device module through Google Play Services; that download is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. The app does not send camera images to us.

4Backups and exports

Backups and reports are created only when you ask for them. You choose the destination with Android's system file picker (for example, Downloads, a cloud drive app or an SD card). Backup archives are ZIP files that contain your database content and attachments.

Backups are not encrypted by the app. Anyone with access to the backup file can read its contents. Store backups somewhere you trust and protect them with your device or cloud account security.

Exported PDF and CSV files are written to the location you choose and may be shared using Android's share sheet. Once a file leaves the app, the receiving app's privacy practices apply.

5Purchases

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details; the app never receives your card number or billing address. The app receives the product identifier, purchase state and a purchase token so that Pro can be restored on your Google account. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6Notifications

Warranty reminders are scheduled locally with Android WorkManager. The app does not use a push notification service. Notification permission is requested only when you enable reminders, and you can turn reminders off at any time in Settings or in Android system settings.

8Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services:

  • Google Play Billing — to offer and restore the optional Pro purchase.
  • Google Play Services code scanner (ML Kit) — to read barcodes on device.

These services may process limited technical information under Google's own privacy policy. We do not integrate advertising networks, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, social login or cloud storage.

9Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual items, photos and documents in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Because we do not hold a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Backup files you created remain in the location you chose until you delete them. Deleting the app does not delete those files.

10Security

The app stores its database and attachments in Android's app-private storage, protected by the operating system's sandbox. Backup files are protected only by the storage location you choose. Keep your device lock enabled and store backups in a trusted account.

11Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information at all, no special children's data practices apply.

12International users

The app does not transfer your inventory content internationally because it does not transmit it. Google Play Billing may process purchase information in the country where you use Google Play, under Google's terms.

13Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and revise the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing and, where appropriate, in the app.

2您在应用中创建的信息

您可能输入物品名称、价格、购买日期、序列号、保修详情、备注、照片和文件。这些内容存储在应用在本设备上的私有存储和本地数据库中。

我们无法访问这些内容。它们不会被上传给我们或任何第三方。如果您卸载应用或清除其存储，内容会从设备上删除。

3照片、文件与相机

您可以为物品添加照片和文件。应用会将您选中的文件复制到其私有存储中。应用不会在后台扫描您的相册。

条码扫描使用 Google Play 服务的本地条码扫描器（ML Kit）。相机只在扫描器界面打开时使用，扫描结果保存在本地。扫描器可能通过 Google Play 服务下载本地模块，该下载由 Google 按其隐私政策处理。应用不会将相机图像发送给我们。

4备份与导出

备份和报告只会在您主动要求时创建。您通过 Android 系统文件选择器（如「下载」文件夹、云盘或 SD 卡）选择保存位置。备份归档为 ZIP 文件，包含数据库内容和附件。

应用不对备份进行加密。任何能访问备份文件的人都可以读取其内容。请将备份存放在您信任的位置，并用设备或云账号的安全机制保护它们。

导出的 PDF 和 CSV 文件写入您选择的位置，并可通过 Android 分享菜单共享。文件一旦离开应用，接收方应用的隐私规则即适用。

5购买

Pro 为一次性、非消耗型应用内购，由 Google Play 计费处理。Google 处理支付细节；应用绝不会收到您的卡号或账单地址。应用仅收到商品标识符、购买状态和购买令牌，以便在您的 Google 账号下恢复 Pro。Google 对购买数据的处理见 Google 隐私政策。

6通知

保修提醒使用 Android WorkManager 在本地排程，应用不使用推送通知服务。通知权限仅在您开启提醒时申请，您随时可以在「设置」或 Android 系统设置中关闭。

9数据保留与删除

您的数据保留在您的设备上，直到您删除。您可以在应用内删除单个物品、照片和文件，也可在 Android 设置中清除应用存储或卸载应用来删除全部数据。由于我们不保留任何副本，服务器上没有需要删除的内容。

您创建的备份文件保留在您选择的位置，直到您手动删除。删除应用不会删除这些文件。

10安全

应用将数据库和附件存储在 Android 应用私有存储中，受系统沙箱保护。备份文件仅受您所选存储位置保护。请保持设备锁定开启，并将备份存放在可信账号中。

11儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，且不主动收集儿童个人信息。由于应用根本不收集个人信息，没有特别的儿童数据规则适用。

12国际用户

应用不会将您的资产内容传输到国外，因为它根本不传输这些数据。Google Play 计费可能会在您使用 Google Play 的国家/地区处理购买信息，受 Google 条款约束。

13对本政策的修改

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们将更新本政策并修订日期。重大变更会反映在应用的商店列表中，并在适当时反映在应用内。
